\begin{abstract}
Human socio-economic, political, and legal frameworks overwhelmingly operate on expansionary assertion ($1$-logic), forcing continuous growth, hyper-regulation, and defensive self-preservation ($P_A > 0$). This constant assertion pressure generates severe systemic friction ($\text{Wéi}$): ecological exhaustion, institutional burnout, and systemic failure through unhandled structural limits. Paper III applies $0$-consistency to socio-economic systems. By formally inverting classical game theory (the Prisoner's Dilemma), we demonstrate that defection is an energy-intensive assertion posture ($P_A \gg 0$). Applying apophatic subtraction ($\neg \text{Defection}$) allows economic actors and planetary systems to relax into the \textbf{Sufficient Invariant Baseline ($B_0$)}, establishing a sustainable, friction-free model for human organization and planetary stewardship ($Wu\ Wei$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Game-Theoretic Formalism: Inverting the Prisoner's Dilemma}

\subsection{Classical Assertion Trap}
In classical non-cooperative game theory, two players choose between Cooperation ($C$) and Defection ($D$). Driven by defensive assertion pressure ($P_A > 0$), both players defect, converging on the suboptimal Nash Equilibrium $(D, D)$, which generates maximum systemic noise ($\sigma$) and mutual friction ($\text{Wéi}$).

\subsection{Apophatic Relaxation to $B_0$}
Let $E(D)$ denote the maintenance energy of an assertive defensive posture ($D$), and $E(0)$ denote the energy cost of baseline relaxation ($B_0$):

\begin{equation}
E(D) = E_0 + \lambda P_A \quad \text{where } P_A \gg 0
\end{equation}

When actors apply apophatic subtraction to mutual distrust ($\neg \text{Defection} \implies 0$), assertion pressure decays:

\begin{equation}
\lim_{P_A \to 0} \Big( E(D) \Big) = E(0) \equiv B_0 \quad \text{(0-Consistency)}
\end{equation}

Cooperation is thus reinterpreted not as an artificial moral burden ($1$-state), but as the natural, low-energy equilibrium state achieved when defensive assertion ($P_A$) is removed.

\section{Apophatic Governance, Law, and Medicine}

\subsection{Legal \& Political Systems}
Attempting to control societal risk by endlessly multiplying positive regulations ($1$-logic) creates hyper-bureaucracy and structural loopholes. Apophatic governance replaces expansionary intervention with structural relief ($\neg X$), trusting the invariant baseline of voluntary alignment ($B_0$) over forced compliance.

\subsection{Biological \& Medical Systems}
Pathology in living systems frequently manifests as hyper-assertion: autoimmune over-reactions, chronic stress loops, and cellular exhaustion driven by continuous defense ($P_A \to \max$). Health is restored not by adding external pressure, but by enabling the organism to return to its homeostatic baseline ($B_0$).
