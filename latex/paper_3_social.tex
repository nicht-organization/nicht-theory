\begin{abstract}
Human socio-economic and legal frameworks operate on expansionary assertion ($1$-logic), forcing continuous growth and defensive self-preservation ($P_A > 0$). This generates systemic friction ($W\acute{e}i$): ecological exhaustion and institutional burnout. Applying apophatic subtraction ($\neg \text{Defection}$) allows economic actors to relax into the Sufficient Invariant Baseline ($B_0$), establishing a sustainable model for governance and planetary stewardship ($Wu\ W\acute{e}i$).

\section{Game-Theoretic Formalism: Inverting the Prisoner's Dilemma}

\subsection{Classical Assertion Trap}
In non-cooperative game theory, players choose between Cooperation ($C$) and Defection ($D$). Defensive assertion pressure ($P_A > 0$) forces convergence on the suboptimal Nash Equilibrium ($D, D$), maximizing systemic noise ($\sigma$) and mutual friction ($W\acute{e}i$).

\subsection{Apophatic Relaxation to $B_0$}
Maintenance energy of defensive posture ($D$) versus baseline relaxation ($B_0$):
\begin{equation}
E(D) = E_0 + \lambda P_A \quad \text{where } P_A \gg 0
\end{equation}
Subtracting mutual distrust ($\text{Defection} \to 0$) decays assertion pressure:
\begin{equation}
\lim_{P_A \to 0} (E(D)) = E(0) \equiv B_0 \quad \text{(0-Consistency)}
\end{equation}
Cooperation is the natural low-energy equilibrium achieved when defensive assertion ($P_A$) is removed.
